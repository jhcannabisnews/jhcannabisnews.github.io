About the show

J&H Cannabis News is a daily news show hosted by James & Hope. We cover the law side of cannabis — what legislatures pass, what courts rule, what agencies enforce, and what voters decide — so you actually understand what's happening, not just the headlines.

Every episode ends the same way: what it means for you, whether you're a consumer, a shop owner, or a voter. New episodes daily. We'll see you at the next ruling.

Who we are

J&H Cannabis News is run by James & Hope, a real husband and wife in Texas who follow cannabis law every day. We read the bills, court rulings and news ourselves, pick the stories, and check the facts.

🎙️ We use AI voices to narrate our episodes so we can publish fast, every weekday. The research, the stories and the choices are ours.
