EPISODE 31 · NOVEMBER 13, 2026

Can you still get THC in Texas after Dec 11? What stays legal

On December 11, 2026, a federal spending law caps finished hemp products at 0.4 milligrams of total THC per container. The Texas Legislature meets January 12, 32 days later. In between, here is the shelf in three columns: what fits under both laws, what is unsettled, and a prescription lane most people have never looked at.

The rule that splits the shelf

From December 11, a finished hemp product with more than 0.4 mg of total THC in one container is no longer hemp under federal law. Container means the whole package, not the serving. A Stafford, Texas shop with about 50 products has pulled an estimated 20 to 40, the owner told KPRC. Texas elected a lieutenant governor who says she is not interested in a full ban on THC, but she takes office in January, and nothing decided on November 3 moves the federal date.

Column one: under both laws

Products at or under 0.4 mg of total THC for the whole container, mostly CBD isolate, should fit under both laws. One caution: the FDA's list of similar cannabinoids is still missing.

Column two: unsettled

This is the gray area. Delta-9 gummies and drinks that pass Texas's 0.3% test are in it. Texas's hemp rule is still on the books. From December 11, federal law does not treat a product over the cap as hemp. We found no ruling on whether a Texas shop can keep selling it. For your own shop, ask a lawyer.

Delta-8 and other converted THCs have been controlled in Texas since July 31. THCA counts as total THC under both the state rules and the federal rule.

Column three: the prescription lane

The Texas Compassionate Use Program is a state medical registry. DPS's October 2026 report lists 180,299 patients listed by their physicians since 2017. That is a lifetime count, not people shopping today. It was 138,567 in January. DPS counts 989 approved physicians, out of roughly 80,000 doctors in Texas as of January.

  • Who qualifies. The condition list includes cancer, epilepsy, PTSD, multiple sclerosis, traumatic brain injury, Crohn's and terminal illness. Chronic pain counts: severe pain lasting more than 90 days from a chronic condition that other treatments have not relieved.
  • How it works. You must be a permanent Texas resident. Per DPS, you do not register with the state or pay it a fee. A registered doctor enters the prescription in the registry, and you pick it up with your ID.
  • The limits. Under HB 46, up to 10 milligrams of THC per dose. The Texas Tribune put products at $40 to $70 in January.

On the federal side, the Justice Department moved marijuana under a state medical license to Schedule III in April, and on October 30 announced the DEA had begun registering dispensaries in seven states, Texas among them, Fox 7 reported.

The four locks

When could the rest come back? Four locks.

  1. The health department's rules. They are in effect while the appeal waits. The 15th Court of Appeals canceled its Dec 8 hemp-rules argument, to be reset at a later date, the Texas Tribune reported. Three new justices take office in early January. We found no argument date and no ruling date. One CannaWize analyst estimates a rule rewrite would land around the end of April or May, and says nobody has announced a plan.
  2. The delta-8 schedule. CannaWize says undoing it takes a health department decision after a public hearing.
  3. State law. The session opens January 12, 2027. Filing closes March 12 and the session ends around May 31. A new law usually takes effect September 1, unless two-thirds of both chambers vote for sooner.
  4. The federal date. December 11 is federal, and only Congress and the president can move it. Rep. Morgan Griffith wants his hemp regulation bill in a budget package before then, and Sen. Rand Paul wants another extension, Townhall reported. We found no vote.

Goodwin and Virdell

At her first press conference on Tuesday, Nov 10, Lt. Gov.-elect Vikki Goodwin said: "I think we need regulation around THC." And: "I'm not interested in a full ban on THC, but there's a lot of details to be worked out." She did not commit to making legalization a priority, the San Antonio Current reported.

Rep. Wes Virdell (R-Kerrville) told KXAN that cannabis is an issue where he and the Democrats "can definitely align." He predicted bipartisan bills. By his account, he voted for the 2025 ban after Lt. Gov. Patrick promised to expand the medical program, and later suggested to the governor's team that he veto it.

Hemp ban bills filed: zero that we found

As of our 5 AM check on Friday, Nov 13, we found no hemp ban or hemp regulation bill among 1,121 House and 336 Senate filings. Filing runs to March 12, 2027, so that can change. If a hemp bill is filed, the court sets a date, or Congress votes, we will say so.
