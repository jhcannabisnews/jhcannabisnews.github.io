📞 Contact Your Texas Lawmakers

The 2027 session starts January 12. Lawmakers count every call and email from people in their own district. It takes about five minutes. Here's how.

1️⃣ Find your two lawmakers

Every Texan has one Texas House member and one state senator. Type your address into the Texas Legislature's official lookup and it shows both, with their office phone numbers.

Find my lawmakers (official site) →

That's the Legislature's own site. We never see your address.

2️⃣ Call (about 30 seconds)

"Hi, my name is ______ and I live in ______, in the district. I'm calling about cannabis. I'd like [Representative / Senator] ______ to support regulating it instead of banning it: lab testing, clear labels, 21 and over, and keeping it away from kids. Please support HB 362, HB 369 and SB 237. Thank you!"

A staffer usually answers. Be polite, give your city, and you're done. Add your own reason if you want: medical, a family business, or your job.

3️⃣ Or send an email

Subject: Please regulate cannabis, don't ban it

Dear [Representative / Senator] ______,

I live in ______, in your district. I'm asking you to support regulating cannabis in Texas instead of banning it: lab testing, clear labels, 21 and over, and keeping it out of kids' hands. Please support HB 362, HB 369 and SB 237 in the 2027 session.

Why it matters to me: ______

Thank you for your time,
______

Their email or contact form is on their page in the official lookup above.

📜 The bills to name

  • HB 362 and HB 369 (Texas House)
  • SB 237 (Texas Senate)

Filed is not law yet. Follow every cannabis and hemp bill on our Texas bill tracker.
