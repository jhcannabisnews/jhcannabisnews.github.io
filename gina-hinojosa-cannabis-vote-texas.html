EPISODE 34 · MON NOV 16, 2026

Gina Hinojosa wants a cannabis vote. Can she get it?

Texas elected its first Democratic governor in more than three decades (results are preliminary until certified). Gov.-elect Hinojosa says Texans should vote on cannabis. A governor cannot do that alone, and one number explains why.

The number: Republicans will hold 19 of the 31 Texas Senate seats, and under current Senate rules it takes 18 votes just to bring a bill up for debate. Even a regular bill needs some Republican senators on board.

What a governor can and can't do

A governor can sign or veto a bill but cannot pass one. In a video after the election, Hinojosa promised "the people of Texas a right to vote on the future of THC," according to KSAT. But Texas has no citizen petitions. A statewide vote needs a constitutional amendment, and that takes two thirds of both the House and Senate first; amendments do not need the governor's signature.

The Texas Tribune reports Democrats won majority control of the House. In the Senate, Lt. Gov.-elect Vikki Goodwin, who says she is not in favor of a THC ban, will preside after beating Dan Patrick, who backed the 2025 THC ban that Gov. Abbott vetoed. That changes who presides, not the vote count.

The calendar

The session opens January 12, 2027, most bills must be filed by March 12, and it can run 140 days, to May 31. Once a bill reaches the governor during the session she has ten days, not counting Sundays, to sign or veto it; a veto can be overridden by two thirds of both chambers. The Texas Hemp Business Council's Mark Bordas put the chance of full-range THC products returning at "around seven out of 10." That is his guess, not a promise.

What Fox News asked, and the doctor's points checked

On Saturday, Fox and Friends Weekend ran a segment titled "Democrats push for cannabis legalization after midterm victories," and senior medical analyst Dr. Marc Siegel made the case against. We checked several of his points. Potency is up: federal testing found about 4 percent THC in seized cannabis in 1995 and about 16 percent in 2022. A multi-country study in The Lancet Psychiatry found people who used high-potency cannabis daily had nearly five times the odds of a psychotic disorder, a link rather than proof of cause. The 60-million figure is real (about 62 million past-year users in 2023), but only about 18 million were daily or near-daily users in 2022. On pregnancy, national surveys put past-month use near 6 to 7 percent, not 10, and doctors still advise against it.

Four other states that moved this month

  • Pennsylvania: Democrats appear to have taken the state Senate. A bipartisan bill from Sen. Sharif Street (D) and Sen. Dan Laughlin (R) would allow adults 21+ up to 30 grams. Republican Senate leader Joe Pittman says it should not move without more federal changes.
  • Wisconsin: Democrats won both chambers and the governorship. The likely Assembly speaker, Greta Neubauer, says they will legalize "in the coming year to two years."
  • New Hampshire: Democrats won both chambers; Rep. Jared Sullivan is filing a bill. Gov. Kelly Ayotte (R) has long opposed it, and a veto override takes two thirds.
  • Iowa: Gov.-elect Rob Sand wants to legalize, but Republicans kept the legislature. He says "we have to negotiate."

In each state the governor matters, but the legislature decides. Massachusetts voters, meanwhile, rejected a ballot question to repeal that state's legalization law.

Where Texas really stands

Three legalization bills are filed: HB 362 and HB 369 (adults 21+, 2 ounces, 12 plants per home, 20 percent retail tax) and SB 237 (2.5 ounces, counties could ban stores). In Washington, Texas's Senator-elect James Talarico (D) said "I hope we can do that together" about working with President Trump; that is a hope, not a bill. Frequently asked questions

Can Gina Hinojosa legalize cannabis in Texas by herself?

No. A governor can sign or veto bills but cannot pass them. A statewide vote needs a constitutional amendment passed by two thirds of both chambers.

How many Texas Senate votes are needed to bring up a bill?

Under current Senate rules, 18 of 31. Republicans will hold 19 seats.

When does the Texas Legislature meet next?

The 90th session opens January 12, 2027 and can run 140 days, to May 31.

Which states moved on cannabis this month?

Pennsylvania, Wisconsin, New Hampshire and Iowa, where Democratic wins revived legalization efforts. In each, the legislature still decides.
