EPISODE 37 · THU NOV 19, 2026

The plan to stop the hemp ban is here. There's a catch for THCA flower

Two senators, one from each party, filed the Hemp Guardrails Act to replace the Dec 11 hemp ban with rules. We read all 17 pages. Here is what it does, what it leaves out, and what it would mean for flower, gummies and drinks.

Filed is not law. Sen. Tim Sheehy (R-MT) and Sen. Amy Klobuchar (D-MN) filed the bill on Tuesday. We found no committee action on it. Dec 11, when the federal hemp ban starts, is also when government funding runs out.

What the Hemp Guardrails Act does

  • Moves the hemp line. From 0.3 percent total THC to 1 percent. Total THC still counts THCA.
  • Scraps the 0.4 mg per-container cap. In its place, final products can't have more than trace amounts of "restricted cannabinoids": delta-8, delta-10, HHC and THC-O, plus their isomers and analogs. CBD, CBN, CBG and delta-9 are not in that class. The bill sets no milligram limit for gummies or drinks and does not say whether the FDA would set one.
  • Adults only. Sales to, and possession by, anyone under 21 would be prohibited.
  • FDA rules. The FDA would have 180 days to write rules for packaging, labels, manufacturing and testing, with child-resistant packaging required. Products would have to be grown, made and packaged in the U.S.

It would start Dec 11, the same day the ban does. Sheehy said it "will put every bad actor in the hemp space out of business." Klobuchar said the looming ban threatens farmers, small businesses and consumers.

The veterans letter

The same day, 27 veterans groups and veteran-owned businesses sent congressional leaders a letter urging them to regulate hemp rather than eliminate it. Signers include the VFW, the Non Commissioned Officers Association, Burn Pits 360 and a Texas post, VFW Sam Houston Post 76. They say they share the goals behind the ban, keeping products from kids and ending an unregulated market, but that "a blanket ban does not achieve either." They ask for a minimum age of 21, third-party testing, accurate labels, child-resistant packaging, clear serving limits and a federal regulator.

The letter does not name the Guardrails Act. It says no signer was paid for its support, but at least one signer is a hemp business, and Marijuana Moment reported the VFW has a licensing deal with Torch, which sells hemp THC drinks. That does not make the letter wrong; it is context you can weigh.

Can it pass in 22 days?

Rep. James Comer (R-KY) told Spectrum News he does not expect a standalone hemp bill to get a vote before Dec 11: "The best opportunity, in my opinion, is to get this on a bill that is going to pass." That means the next funding bill. Nobody has announced hemp language is going into it. Ban backers can also try to keep hemp out, or push the date further. So there are three paths: the ban starts Dec 11, Congress delays it again, or rules like this ride along in the funding deal.

The catch for THCA flower

Even if the bill passed tomorrow, it would not save everything. Delta-8 is a named restricted cannabinoid. The bill's definition of hemp products leaves out raw hemp flower and similar flower products, and it would make shipping them across state lines a prohibited act. And total THC counts THCA: under the USDA formula, delta-9 plus about 88 percent of the THCA. Flower at 15 percent THCA comes out near 13 percent total; at 30 percent, near 26. Against a 1 percent line, typical THCA flower would be 13 to 26 times over, so it would not be hemp under this bill or under the ban. States could still be stricter inside their own borders.

Winners: hemp farmers (a higher line) and makers of drinks, gummies and CBD, who could stay on the market under FDA rules. Losers: shops built on THCA flower, delta-8 and HHC sellers, and sellers of imported products.

What it means for Texas

Some of the catch is already Texas law. Delta-8 and other converted THC have been controlled substances here since July 31, after a Texas Supreme Court ruling in May, and Texas health department rules count THCA toward total THC while the court fight continues. The bill lets states be stricter, so Texas could still write its own rules when the Legislature returns January 12.

Both sides

Ban backers say the 2018 Farm Bill opened a loophole Congress never meant to create, pointing to strong products in gas stations, candy-like packaging and untested products, and argue for closing it now. Regulation backers say a ban pushes buyers to an unregulated market with no age checks, and that age limits, testing and packaging protect kids better. Call your U.S. representative and both senators before Dec 11.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Hemp Guardrails Act?

A bipartisan Senate bill from Sen. Tim Sheehy (R-MT) and Sen. Amy Klobuchar (D-MN) that would replace the Dec 11 hemp ban with FDA rules, raise the hemp line to 1% total THC and require buyers to be 21+.

Would the bill keep THCA flower legal?

Not typical flower. Total THC counts THCA, so most flower would be far over the 1% line, and the bill leaves raw flower products out of its definition.

Would it keep the 0.4 mg per-container cap?

No. It scraps the cap and sets no new milligram number.

Has the bill passed?

No. It was filed Nov 17 and we found no committee action.
