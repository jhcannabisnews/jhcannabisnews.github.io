Is delta-8 legal in Alabama?

For now, we couldn't confirm a statewide delta-8 ban in Alabama, but the federal hemp ban reaches every state on Nov 12 and Dec 11, 2026.

⚠️ Delta-8 & hemp THC: No statewide ban confirmed, for now. We couldn't confirm a statewide ban in Alabama, but rules vary and change often, and the federal changes on Nov 12 and Dec 11 apply here too. Check before you buy.

The latest in Alabama

Medical program licensed; recreational use is illegal.

Marijuana in Alabama

Recreational marijuana is illegal. There is a medical marijuana program for qualifying patients.

The federal hemp ban: Nov 12 and Dec 11, 2026

The federal hemp changes apply in every state: on Nov 12, 2026, cannabinoids the plant can't make naturally (like THC-O) lose federal hemp status, and on Dec 11, 2026, a finished hemp product with more than 0.4 mg of total THC per container (THCA counts) is no longer hemp under federal law, unless Congress changes it. Analysts disagree on whether delta-8 made from CBD is affected Nov 12 or Dec 11, so don't count on the later date.
