Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Who we are

J&H Cannabis News is run by James & Hope, a husband and wife in Texas. Questions about this policy: james@jhcannabisnews.com.

Our website

We don't sell anything on this site, we don't run ads, and we don't use advertising cookies.

  • Visitor counts: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and only gives us totals (like page views). It doesn't identify you.
  • Videos: embedded videos play through YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube's own privacy policy applies when you play them.
  • Ask James & Hope form: if you send a question, it goes to a Google Form we own. You choose what to share (a first name or nickname, your state, your question, and an optional email so we can follow up). We only use it to answer questions on the show or reply to you, and we never sell or share it.
  • Links: we link to other sites like YouTube, Discord, our merch stores (Printify and Fourthwall) and government sites. Each has its own privacy policy.

YouTube comments

We read the public comments on our own YouTube videos so we can reply to viewers and pick questions for our Sunday Q&A.

JH Toolbox (our private internal tool)

JH Toolbox is a private tool we use only for our own channels. It is not offered to the public.

  • With our own permission, it reads our own YouTube channels' analytics (such as views, watch time, audience retention and click-through rate) through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API, read-only.
  • We use it only to improve our own videos. The data stays on our own computer. It is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • It cannot post, upload, edit or delete anything.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • JH Toolbox's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • JH Toolbox uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
