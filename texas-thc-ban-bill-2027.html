EPISODE 29 · NOVEMBER 11, 2026

Texas marijuana bills are filed. Where's the THC ban bill?

As of Tuesday afternoon, Texas lawmakers had filed 1,354 bills and resolutions for the 2027 session. Hemp THC ban bills we found: zero. Here's what that does and doesn't mean, the hemp court hearing that was canceled, and the federal date no Texas bill can stop.

Up front: delta-8 has been a controlled substance in Texas since July 31, 2026. Nothing filed so far changes that. Filed is not law, marijuana is still illegal in Texas, and bills can be filed until March 12, 2027, so a zero today isn't a zero forever.

1. The zero

We searched the captions of every filing in the House (1,045) and Senate (309) filed-bill reports as of Tuesday afternoon, Nov 10, for hemp, THC, cannabinoid, consumable and related terms. Six filings touch cannabis, and none is a ban bill.

  • Three would make marijuana legal for adults under state law: HB 362, HB 369 and SB 237. All three leave hemp out of their definition of cannabis.
  • One medical-program bill (HB 330), one public-job drug-test bill (HB 507) and one on marijuana concentrate penalties (HB 667).

Senator Charles Perry said in July that he would file another ban, KSAT reported. As of Tuesday afternoon he had filed 17 bills, on topics like water and youth camps, and none on hemp that we found. We found no new statements on a ban from state leaders.

2. The court hearing that was canceled

The Fifteenth Court of Appeals canceled its Dec 8 argument in the industry's challenge to the state's hemp rules, the Texas Tribune reported Nov 6. It came after voters chose three new justices on Nov 3, who take office in early January, and the Tribune says the case will be reset "at a later date." Among other things, those rules raised the manufacturer fee from $258 to $10,000 per facility, and retail registration from $155 to $5,000. Nobody has ruled yet.

3. The federal date no Texas bill can stop

Thursday, Nov 12: federal law starts treating certain lab-made cannabinoids, the ones the plant can't naturally make, as marijuana. Lawyers disagree about whether that includes delta-8.

Dec 11: a cap of 0.4 mg of total THC per container (the innermost retail package). Total THC counts THCA. Products over the cap are treated as marijuana under federal law.

Congress delayed most of these rules from Nov 12 to Dec 11 in a bill signed Sept 2. As of Tuesday we found no vote and no bill text for another delay. Read the full background in our federal hemp ban explainer.

How Texas got here

  • June 2025: Governor Abbott vetoed SB 3, a total ban on consumable hemp THC.
  • Summer 2025: in special sessions, Perry's ban bills passed the Senate; the House never passed one.
  • Sept 2025: Abbott ordered state agencies to regulate hemp THC instead.
  • March 31, 2026: the health department's rules took effect, then went to court.
  • July 31, 2026: after a May Texas Supreme Court ruling, delta-8 and similar isomers became controlled substances.

Veterans Day: two facts

PTSD has been a qualifying condition in Texas's low-THC medical program since Sept 1, 2021, for any patient with a diagnosis. And the House adult-use bills filed Monday would send 25% of the new cannabis tax to the Fund for Veterans' Assistance.
