⚖ The Watch List

Every date that matters in cannabis law right now. Updated with every episode.

NOV 3, 2026 · RESULTS

Election results (unofficial) — Massachusetts rejected Question 8 (~76% no), so legal sales stay. Idaho rejected HJR 4 (~67% no). Houston approved Prop A (~72% yes). In Texas, Vikki Goodwin beat Lt. Gov. Dan Patrick (~52-46), who led the 2025 THC ban push; Patrick conceded. Gina Hinojosa won governor and the Texas House is projected to flip; the Texas Senate is projected to stay Republican. Nothing on Texas shelves changes until lawmakers or the state act. Ep 22

NOV 5, 2026

Federal — optional reply briefs due in the DEA marijuana rescheduling hearing. No date yet for the judge's recommendation.

NOV 9, 2026

Texas + Federal — early bill filing opens for the 2027 Texas session, and the outgoing Congress returns for its lame-duck session (the only Congress that can act before Dec 11). Follow every cannabis, hemp and THC bill on our Texas bill tracker.

NOV 12, 2026

Federal — cannabinoids the plant can't make naturally (like THC-O) lose federal hemp status in all 50 states. Most other changes, likely including delta-8, moved to Dec 11.

DEC 11, 2026

Federal — the 0.4 mg total-THC-per-container cap hits natural hemp products. Industry lawyers estimate ~95% of hemp cannabinoid products become federally unlawful.

JAN 3, 2027

Federal — the new Congress is sworn in. Networks project a new majority, but it arrives after the Dec 11 hemp change.

2027 SESSION

Texas — Sen. Charles Perry has said he'll refile the total THC ban when the legislature returns.
