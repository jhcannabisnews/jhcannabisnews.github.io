⚖ The Watch List

Every date that matters in cannabis law right now. Updated with every episode.

OCT 30, 2026

Texas — Travis County District Court hearing on the state THC ban. The industry's next shot at an injunction.

NOV 3, 2026

Election Day — Massachusetts votes on Question 8 (re-banning recreational sales). Idaho votes on a constitutional amendment blocking citizen legalization initiatives.

NOV 12, 2026

Federal — cannabinoids the plant can't make naturally (like THC-O) lose federal hemp status in all 50 states. Most other changes, likely including delta-8, moved to Dec 11.

DEC 11, 2026

Federal — the 0.4 mg total-THC-per-container cap hits natural hemp products. Industry lawyers estimate ~95% of hemp cannabinoid products become federally unlawful.

2027 SESSION

Texas — Sen. Charles Perry has said he'll refile the total THC ban when the legislature returns.
