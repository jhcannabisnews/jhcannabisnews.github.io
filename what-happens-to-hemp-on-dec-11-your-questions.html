EPISODE 40 · SUN NOV 22, 2026

What happens to your hemp on Dec 11? Your questions, answered

Dec 11 is 19 days away. We read the law and bill text to answer viewer questions: hemp seeds, CBD, flower by mail, where the THC in a hemp drink would come from, what happens to what you already own, and the real penalty for possession in Texas.

The line in the law: from Dec 11, hemp counts total THC including THCA, and a finished product can't have more than 0.4 mg of THC and similar cannabinoids combined in the whole container (P.L. 119-37, Sec. 781).

Hemp seeds: are they affected?

We read Section 781. Hemp includes the seeds of the plant, as long as the plant is at or under 0.3 percent total THC; viable seeds from a plant over that line are excluded. The law also lists the whole grain, oil, cake, nut and hull of hemp seeds as industrial hemp, which is not counted as a cannabinoid product, so the new cannabinoid limits do not apply to it. The seeds in your cereal are not what Dec 11 is aimed at.

Why does the limit catch CBD?

The plant is not banned: a plant at or under 0.3 percent total THC is still hemp. The limit that bites is on finished products, no more than 0.4 mg of THC and similar cannabinoids per container. Full-spectrum CBD keeps the small amounts of THC the plant makes; across a whole bottle, just 1 mg already more than doubles the cap. CBD isolate, made without THC, should land under it. The Sheehy-Klobuchar bill would scrap the per-container cap and use a 1 percent plant line, with no more than trace amounts of delta-8 and similar compounds.

Where would the THC in a hemp drink come from?

Under current law, an intermediate product such as an extract with more than 0.3 percent combined THC and similar cannabinoids is not hemp, and products with cannabinoids made outside the plant are excluded. The Sheehy-Klobuchar bill adds a lane for an "unfinished hemp cannabinoid ingredient" (oil, extract, concentrate or distillate) that can go above 1 percent while being processed but must stay with permitted manufacturers and cannot be sold at retail. The text does not say who counts as permitted; the FDA would write manufacturing, testing and registration rules within 180 days of enactment. One viewer's estimate that farmers would need far more plants is his estimate, not the bill's.

Flower by mail

Flower counts as hemp only if the plant is at or under 0.3 percent total THC, and from Dec 11 THCA counts. Much flower sold today is far above that, so it stops being hemp, and the hemp rules that let it ship are gone. The Sheehy-Klobuchar bill would not bring that back: it also bars hemp flower from interstate commerce. For a viewer on disability who told us price and access are the whole story, neither path helps flower by mail.

What happens to products you already own?

We found no grace period in Section 781 and no clause letting existing stock keep selling, and Spectrum News reported no grace period for businesses with leftover inventory. On what is already in your home, we found nothing in the text either way and nothing on enforcement; that is a question for a lawyer. Congress could still change things, because Dec 11 is also the day government funding runs out.

Texas penalties, and policing a regulated market

In Texas, two ounces or less of marijuana is a Class B misdemeanor: up to 180 days in jail and a $2,000 fine. A 2024 Lancet Public Health review of 17 earlier analyses found high rates of mental and physical health problems among people in prison, with authors saying better long-term studies are needed; we found no number for a short jail stay. Asked who would police every store, the Sheehy-Klobuchar text gives the FDA rules for child-resistant packaging, a QR code or web link to third-party lab results, manufacturing standards, registration and record keeping, plus inspection and seizure powers. We found nothing on inspector numbers or cost.

The bill, filed Nov 17, has no committee vote we could find. Sens. Ted Budd (R-NC) and Pete Ricketts (R-NE) say they will not support any year-end package that delays or builds rules around these products. See that story. Regulate, Don't Ban.

Frequently asked questions

Are hemp seeds affected by the Dec 11 hemp change?

No. Hemp seeds, grain, oil, cake, nut and hull are industrial hemp, and the new cannabinoid limits do not apply to them.

Will CBD products be affected on Dec 11?

Full-spectrum CBD can fail if the whole container holds more than 0.4 mg of THC and similar cannabinoids. CBD isolate made without THC should land under it.

Is there a grace period for products already on shelves?

We found none in Section 781, and Spectrum News reported no grace period for leftover inventory.

What is the penalty for 2 ounces or less of marijuana in Texas?

A Class B misdemeanor: up to 180 days in jail and a $2,000 fine.
